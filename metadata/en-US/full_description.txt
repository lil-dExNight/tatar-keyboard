A native Android keyboard (IME) where Tatar is a first-class language: the standard ЙЦУКЕН layout plus a dedicated, always-visible fifth row for <b>ә ө ү җ ң һ</b>. All 39 letters of the Tatar alphabet are one tap away — no long-pressing, no hunting through popups.

To enable the keyboard:

* Open "Tatar Keyboard" from your launcher — a two-step onboarding walks you through enabling and selecting the keyboard
* Or do it manually: system Settings → Languages &amp; input → On-screen keyboard → enable Tatar Keyboard, then switch to it from your current input method (usually via the keyboard icon or long-press space)

<b>Tatar, done properly:</b>

* Tatar layout: ЙЦУКЕН + a visible fifth row with ә ө ү җ ң һ
* Three languages: Tatar, Russian, English — switch with the globe key
* Long-press duplicates on the Russian layout: а→ә, о→ө, у→ү, ж→җ, н→ң, х→һ, plus е→ё and ь→ъ

<b>Word suggestions and next-word prediction (Tatar and Russian):</b>

* As you type, up to three completions appear in the strip above the keyboard (four with the "Four suggestions" setting) — tap to insert
* After a finished word, the strip predicts what comes next: type «мин » and see дә · аны · бу
* Optional autocorrect: a mistyped word is fixed when you type a space; one tap on backspace undoes it
* Suggestions are on by default, can be switched off in settings, and are never shown in password or other private fields
* The dictionaries live on your device — 100,000 word forms per language, plus conversational forms from open subtitle and sentence corpora

<b>Emoji:</b>

* A full emoji panel with categories and recently used emoji
* Search by word in three languages — Tatar included: «этэч» finds 🐓, «көлү» finds 😄
* Emoji suggestions in the strip: type «самолет » or «сәлам » and a matching emoji appears — tapping only adds it, your typed word is never replaced
* Emoji suggestions are on by default and can be switched off in settings

<b>Personal dictionary:</b>

* Optional (off by default): remembers words you type so they can be suggested later
* Every saved word is visible and erasable in settings, per language
* "Erase everything saved" deletes saved words, word pairs, learned emoji, recent emoji, text shortcuts and corrections at once
* "Pause learning" stops the keyboard from remembering anything new without erasing what is saved

<b>Everyday typing:</b>

* Double-space → period, swipe on space to move the cursor, multi-touch
* Key-press preview, long-press alternatives popup, sound and vibration (all configurable)
* Light and dark themes
* Accessibility: full TalkBack support with Tatar letter descriptions
* Works before the first unlock (Direct Boot) — available for PIN entry right after reboot

<b>Privacy:</b>

* No INTERNET permission at all — the keyboard physically cannot send what you type anywhere; this is verified by CI on every commit, on the source manifest and on the built APK
* No ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs
* The only permission is Vibrate
* Nothing is backed up off your device: cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are excluded for all app data
* The personal dictionary and recent-emoji list live in an app folder that is decrypted only after you unlock the device

<b>Lightweight:</b>

* The app is about 1.8 MB — dictionaries, prediction tables and emoji data included

Free and open source under Apache-2.0. A fork of Simple Keyboard, which is based on the AOSP LatinIME keyboard.
