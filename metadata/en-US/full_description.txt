A native Android keyboard (IME) with full Tatar support: the standard ЙЦУКЕН layout plus a dedicated, always-visible fifth row for <b>ә ө ү җ ң һ ё ъ</b>. All 39 letters of the Tatar alphabet are one tap away.

To enable the keyboard:

* Open "Tatar Keyboard" and follow the two-step setup
* Or enable it manually in system Settings → Languages &amp; input → On-screen keyboard, then select it as the current input method

<b>Layout and languages:</b>

* Tatar layout: ЙЦУКЕН + a visible fifth row with ә ө ү җ ң һ ё ъ — every Tatar alphabet letter, ё and ъ included, has its own direct key
* Three languages: Tatar, Russian, English — switch with the globe key
* Long-press duplicates on the Russian layout: а/э→ә, о→ө, у→ү, ж→җ, н→ң, х→һ, plus е→ё and ь→ъ

<b>Word suggestions and next-word prediction (Tatar and Russian):</b>

* Up to three completions appear above the keyboard (four with "Four suggestions") — tap to insert. The strip can also offer Tatar corrections for chat spellings with two or more Russian partner letters; autocorrect never applies them
* After a finished word, the strip predicts what comes next: type «мин » and see дә · аны · бу
* Optional autocorrect: a mistyped word is fixed when you type a space, punctuation or Enter; one tap on backspace undoes it
* Autocorrect leaves a word alone if it is in the other language's dictionary and you have typed in that language since the keyboard started
* Optional Tatar spell checker: select it in Android settings to underline misspelled Tatar words in other apps. Known Russian words and all English words are not underlined; no submitted text is stored or used for learning
* Suggestions are on by default, can be switched off in settings, and are never shown in password or other private fields
* The dictionaries live on your device, with conversational forms from open subtitle and sentence corpora

<b>Emoji:</b>

* A full emoji panel with categories and recently used emoji
* Search by word in three languages — Tatar included: «этэч» finds 🐓, «көлү» finds 😄
* Emoji whose skin-tone versions the system font cannot draw get no tone picker and never appear in search or in the strip
* Emoji suggestions in the strip: type «самолет » or «сәлам » and a matching emoji appears — tapping adds it and keeps the word you typed
* Emoji suggestions are on by default and can be switched off in settings

<b>Personal dictionary:</b>

* On by default, can be switched off in settings: remembers words you type so they can be suggested later
* Every saved word is visible and erasable in settings, per language
* "Erase everything saved" deletes saved words, word pairs, learned emoji, sentence starts, recent emoji, text shortcuts and corrections at once
* "Pause learning" stops the keyboard from remembering anything new without erasing what is saved

<b>Everyday typing:</b>

* Glide typing for Tatar and Russian: slide your finger over the letters; the word is inserted when you lift it, with alternatives in the strip. On by default and can be switched off in settings
* Double-space → period, swipe on space to move the cursor, several simultaneous touches without lost letters
* Key-press preview, long-press alternatives popup, sound and vibration (all configurable)
* Light and dark themes
* Accessibility: full TalkBack support with Tatar letter descriptions
* Works before the first unlock (Direct Boot) — available for PIN entry right after reboot

<b>Privacy:</b>

* No INTERNET permission: the keyboard cannot send what you type; CI checks the source manifest and built APK on every commit
* No ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs
* The only permission is Vibrate
* Nothing is backed up off your device: cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are excluded for all app data
* The personal dictionary and recent-emoji list live in an app folder that is decrypted only after you unlock the device

Open source under Apache-2.0. A fork of Simple Keyboard, which is based on the AOSP LatinIME keyboard.
